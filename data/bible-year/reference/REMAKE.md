Our implementation uses the same measured portrait card geometry, palette, monospaced title, textured background, phrase-caption placement and scripture badge. It substitutes original Bible study illustrations and app download cards. It does not reuse the source narration or document artwork. Exact original glyphs, music, easing curves and source assets remain unknown. The long format adapts the portrait composition to landscape.

Speech: the source voice identity and music provenance are unconfirmed. Automated BPM and sound-effect detections in the generated report are candidates, not verified identifications. OCR was unavailable during this breakdown, so the tool did not recover a complete source caption transcript. New narration uses en-US-GuyNeural with actual returned word-boundary timestamps; phrase captions follow those timestamps. Store QR codes render after the texture pass and were decoded in the preview verification.

The report is a reference analysis, not a claim of pixel-identical reconstruction. Review output clips against the reference when changing the renderer.
